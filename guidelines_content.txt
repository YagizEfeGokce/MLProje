Project Weight: 30 % of Final Grade

1. Project Objective 
The purpose of this project is to apply the concepts learned throughout the course to a real-
world regression or classification problem. 
You will work as a team to design, implement, and evaluate several machine-learning models 
from the course topics. 
By completing this project, you will: 
• Gain experience with the full ML pipeline — data → model → evaluation → 
interpretation. 
• Apply supervised and ensemble methods in practical scenarios. 
• Develop teamwork, communication, and report-writing skills.

3. Project Instructions 
A. Problem Definition 
• Choose a supervised learning problem (regression or classification). 
• Clearly state the goal and why it matters. 
• Make sure the problem is feasible with your resources. 
B. Dataset 
You may use: 
1. Public datasets (Kaggle, UCI, OpenML, Google Dataset Search) 
2. Combined datasets or API-based data collection 
3. Self-collected datasets (eligible for bonus points) 
Your report must include: 
• Dataset source and link 
• Number of instances and features 
• Target variable and problem type 
• Ethical and privacy considerations 
 
C. Data Pre-processing & EDA 
• Handle missing values, duplicates, and outliers. 
• Encode categorical variables and scale numerical ones. 
• Provide visual summaries (correlation plots, histograms, box plots). 
D. Model Implementation 
• Train at least three models from different families (e.g., Decision Tree, Random Forest, 
SVM). 
• Use cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning (GridSearchCV / 
RandomizedSearchCV). 
• Evaluate with appropriate metrics (Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1, RMSE, R², etc.). 
E. Results and Discussion 
• Compare models using tables and graphs. 
• Explain why certain models performed better. 
• Discuss overfitting/underfitting and future improvements. 
F. Report Format 
Your final report ( PDF) must include: 
1. Title of Project 
 
2. Team Members and IDs 
3. Introduction & Motivation 
4. Dataset Description 
5. Methodology (Preprocessing + Algorithms) 
6. Results & Discussion 
7. Conclusion & Future Work 
8. References 
G. Presentation 
• Each team gives a 10-minute presentation in Week 14. 
• All members must participate. 
• Visuals should clearly show key findings and metrics. 
4. Bonus Opportunities 
• +3 % for self-collected or combined datasets. 
• +2 % for creative visualizations or simple UI (e.g., Streamlit dashboard).
